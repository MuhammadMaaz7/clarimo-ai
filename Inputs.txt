## Tips for Testing:

1. **Start with simpler ideas** (like #4 or #5) - they tend to have more focused communities
2. **Use the target market field** - it helps narrow down relevant discussions
3. **Wait for completion** - analysis takes 30-60 seconds
4. **Check all 3 tabs** - Customer Segments, Key Insights, and Communities
5. **Look for relevance** - Communities should be highly relevant to your idea

## Expected Results:

- **Customer Segments**: 2-4 distinct groups with pain points and needs
- **Key Insights**: Main problems, unmet needs, frustrations, and recommendations
- **Communities**: Only highly relevant subreddits with "high" relevance badges
- **Total Discussions**: 15-50 relevant discussions (after AI filtering)

Idea Description

The system analyzes editing style, platform requirements, project type, and deadlines to automatically recommend top editors, shortening hiring time from days to minutes.

Content creators waste time manually searching for editors, reviewing inconsistent portfolios, and negotiating unclear pricing, leading to delayed video production.
